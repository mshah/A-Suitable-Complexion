## Local installation
Unzip then place `a-suitable-complexion/` inside your repository's `exhibitions/` folder, replacing the earlier local exhibition folder. Open `exhibitions/a-suitable-complexion/index.html` to review. No dependencies, build steps, GitHub commits, or deployment changes.

## Rights and interpretation
- Historic ads are documentary evidence; rights and credit notes in archive-notes.
- The Snow White quote refers to a familiar fairy-tale motif. It is an interpretive analogy, not a claim that this story originated Indian complexion hierarchies.
- The complexion key is artist-defined and not a validated measurement instrument.
