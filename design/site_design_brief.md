# Digital Exhibition — Information Architecture and Visual Direction

## Editorial proposition

Build a place for slow looking, not a beauty quiz or a product catalogue. The artworks begin with family intimacy; the essays open onto commerce, history and safety. The site should ask viewers to consider systems without inviting them to classify themselves or anyone else.

## Seven destinations

1. **/** — opening statement, one licensed/permission-cleared finished artwork, and clear routes to three reading paths.
2. **/archive** — family-artwork index, work detail pages with optional permission-cleared originals, provenance and contextual captions.
3. **/timeline** — *From Snow to Glow* interactive but fully readable timeline.
4. **/intimacy-and-commerce** — long essay and two carefully differentiated market data cards.
5. **/the-promise-machine** — advertising archive with typographic slogan interludes and exact sourcing.
6. **/the-price-of-fairness** — accessible ingredient-risk explanation, UK/WHO documentation, source links.
7. **/method-and-ethics** and **/sources** — series protocol, researcher notes, glossary, image rights, accessibility, corrections and contact.

## Visual character

- **Archive and essays:** warm ivory, charcoal typography, soft document-paper details; ample whitespace and a refined serif for long-form headings.
- **Family works:** let the photograph and clothing stay expressive; the identical seven-chip key remains visibly administrative and cool.
- **Data:** editorial infographics, no sensational gauges, no ranking that treats the lightest shade as an endpoint or goal.
- **Advertising:** original layouts and images preserved when allowed; seductive scale and crisp typography, followed by calm critical captions.
- **Safety:** clinically clear, without disfiguring before/after images or dramatized injured faces. Use honest citations and explicitly scoped risks.

## Narrative patterns

- First image → small sentence → generous whitespace → family archive.
- Gradual transition from family colours to archival documents and corporate language.
- Inline **Evidence** notes on every market number, legal rule, and historical slogan.
- A 'View original source' link on each archival record; 'Show label' and 'Why this number?' should be keyboard operable.
- No personalized shade matcher. The artwork critiques classification and should not invite visitors to classify uploaded photos.

## Interaction concepts

**Reveal the imposed key:** toggle the legend beside an image and read the method, without digitally reassessing a person's skin. Original and painted image comparison should only be available when both are cleared.

**Timeline filter:** India / other Asia / global regulation. All entries remain in the DOM/accessible text.

**The promise map:** view the promises grouped by romance, work, class, health and beauty. Every quoted phrase resolves to a documented advert.

**Follow the money:** editorial diagram of a plausible commercial feedback mechanism. Mark 'Interpretive model', not measured causality.

**Market size:** show global skin-lightening revenue estimate separately from India all-skincare estimate; do not place them on the same comparison axis or infer an India subset.

**Safety dossier:** three cards for mercury, hydroquinone and potent topical steroids, with jurisdiction/clinical caveats and primary source links.

## Responsive, accessible site

- Body text approx. 17–20px on desktop with 65–75-character line length; minimum mobile text 16px; line height 1.5+
- WCAG 2.2 AA target; strong focus indicators, semantic headings, visible links and high contrast.
- Artwork color assignment must always be read in a text table; never color alone.
- Reflow every element at 320px width; avoid horizontal scrolling in the timeline by offering a stacked mode.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion and provide no-animation option; no autoplay audio/video.
- For all portraits, alt text describes scene and manipulation rather than diagnosing someone's complexion.
- No analytics tracking minors through their portraits; remove EXIF/geolocation when exporting originals.

## Publishing gates

1. Family permission for each particular image and likeness, including originals if displayed.
2. Image QC: seven exact sRGB swatches, one shade per filled person, numbers only on unfilled skin, no changes to identity/garment.
3. Archive rights and credit checked per item; do not substitute a reproduction pulled from a search result with no rights chain.
4. Citation links current; market figures and forecasts accurately dated; health text expert-reviewed if possible.
5. Artist sign-off on first-person voice, ethics and how relatives are described.
