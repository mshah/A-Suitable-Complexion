## 3. “A market you can measure”

Type: bar chart, provided as `visuals/global_market.svg`.

Data (Grand View Research): 2023 historic estimate US$11.2bn, 2026 projection US$13.2bn, 2030 projection US$16.1bn. APAC estimated 54.8% of the same global category in 2023. The plotted category includes products for dark spots and uneven pigmentation. [S13]

Do **not** place India's entire skincare figure ($2.98bn in 2026 from a different firm) on the same axis. Give it a separately labeled text card: different geography, different product definition, different method. [S14]

## 4. “From Snow to Glow”

Type: timeline module using `data/timeline.json` and `editorial/09_exhibition_timeline.md`.

Era bands: Preindustrial ideals; late colonial / colonial advertising; early independence; mass-market fairness; rebranding; regulation. Every date must retain its caveat. In particular, 1975 is reported test marketing, while a HUL corporate retrospective places launch in 1978. [S07] [S32]

## 5. “Slogans sold as futures”

Type: large, restrained text tiles with a provenance drawer.

Proposed first tile: **WHITE MAKES YOU WIN** — Seoul Secret Snowz (Thailand, 2016), an advertising slogan documented contemporaneously; subsequently withdrawn. [S21]

Other verifiable tiles: **FROM EBONY TO IVORY** — GlutaMAX (Philippines, 2008); **GIVES NATURAL CHARM** — Hazeline Snow (Wellcome, undated); **FAIR & LOVELY → GLOW & LOVELY** — India, 2020 rename. [S25] [S26] [S09]

Never mix slogans in quotation marks with invented paraphrases as though all were historical ad text.

## 6. “The unseen ingredient”

Type: ingredient-risk scroll section.

- Mercury: can harm kidneys and nervous system; illicit/unregulated products may contain it. [S15]
- Hydroquinone: medical pigmentation treatment in some jurisdictions, UK cosmetic prohibition / US prescription vs OTC distinction; inappropriate use risk including ochronosis. [S16] [S19]
- Clobetasol: prescription potent steroid; inappropriate prolonged use associated with skin thinning and endocrine consequences. [S17] [S22]

A UK 2025 report gives a documented product test; do not turn one incident into a global prevalence estimate. [S22]

## 7. “Under the advertisement” archive cards

Each card fields: title, image (only with verified rights), year/date confidence, place, advertiser, original slogan if verified, source link, rights, alt text, editorial interpretation and the exact public-facing credit. Rows in `data/advertising_archive_register.csv` are a scouting inventory, not automatically cleared media.
