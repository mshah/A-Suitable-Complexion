How to read the sources

Academic interpretations, advertising campaign reporting, corporate claims and market-research estimates are distinct kinds of evidence. The numbered source tags throughout this site link here. Not every cited text is a primary archive or a statistically representative study.

ARCHIVAL IMAGE LICENSING

Photographs and advertising images are different rights categories.

Wellcome Collection advertising scans L0032214 and L0032212 are attributed to the Wellcome Collection under CC BY 4.0. The 1929 illustration L0032221 is linked to its item record; the rights for a permanent reproduction of that item still require confirmation. Three contemporary campaign reproductions (Seoul Secret, GlutaMAX and Glow & Lovely) are bundled locally for private critical review only. Their public reproduction rights remain to be assessed.

This private review contains 16 wedding-based paintings: 12 artist-selected finals and four working studies. An artist-selected final image is not necessarily cleared for publication. The original family photographs are not included. Permissions for identifiable portraits, including those depicting children, remain under review.
