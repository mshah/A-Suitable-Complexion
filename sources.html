Evidence & credits

A claim should remain traceable to an archive, study, public authority or clearly labelled estimate.

How to read the sources

Academic interpretations, advertising campaign reporting, corporate claims and market-research estimates are distinct kinds of evidence. The numbered source tags throughout this site link here. Not every cited text is a primary archive or a statistically representative study.

ARCHIVAL IMAGE LICENSING

Photographs and advertising images are different rights categories.

Wellcome Collection advertising scans L0032214 and L0032212 are attributed to the Wellcome Collection under CC BY 4.0. The 1929 illustration L0032221 is linked to its item record; obtain confirmation of item rights before publishing a permanent copy. Three contemporary campaign reproductions (Seoul Secret, GlutaMAX and Glow & Lovely) are bundled locally for private critical review only. Their public reproduction rights remain to be assessed.

Six artist-supplied family painting studies are bundled for private review, not as final or publication-cleared artworks. The original family photographs are not bundled. Do not publish identifiable portraits until the relevant permissions have been reviewed.
