How the Works Are Made

Seven colours, one artificial rule

The series takes its seven terms from a documented 2017 matrimonial search taxonomy: Very Fair, Fair, Wheatish, Wheatish Medium, Wheatish Brown, Wheatish Dark and Dark. The original recorded menu also offered 'Doesn't Matter / Any', which is an option not a colour. S01 S23

The exact colour chips were created for this project and are not historically authenticated skin shades. Each artwork samples a representative midtone at the middle of the nose, then gives every visible patch of that person's skin either one flat shade or blank paper with the two-digit number. This is an arbitrary artistic protocol, not scientific colour analysis or a claim about a person.

There is no 'best' shade. Each swatch must be equal in size, with no arrows suggesting that moving toward one end is improvement. The direction and grouping of the palette mirror the language being critiqued, not a value judgment endorsed by the artist.

Why are some people left blank?

The blanks expose the gap between a person's presence and an arbitrary instruction to complete them. They are not ghosted, erased or absent. Their clothing, hair, facial linework and context remain fully visible. Only exposed skin remains the colour of the warm-ivory surface, with its number present. S01

Do the colours reflect people's actual skin tones?

No. They are forced matches to seven artist-selected swatches based on a reference photograph, whose lighting, camera processing and condition can vary. They should never be used to infer race, caste, ancestry or biological identity.

Why use real family photographs?

Because the system should intrude on relationships that are already visible: care, affection, humour and the particularity of a memory. The people in the work are not examples of a social category. They are the people to whom the category is being unfairly applied.

Did colonialism create colourism in India?

The history is more complicated. Preferences for lighter skin have multiple historical and local roots. Colonial power and commercial advertising helped intensify and monetize particular associations, but the exhibition does not claim a single origin or one uniform practice across South Asia. S02 S03

Are all 'brightening' products unsafe?

No. Some products target specific medical pigmentation concerns and are legally formulated. The health section focuses on documented risks from hazardous substances and illegal products, distinguishing those from ordinary cosmetics and prescribed care. S15 S16 S17

Are the market figures exact?

No. Figures attributed to commercial market-research firms are estimates and forecasts under particular category definitions. They are not audited totals and are not used as if all skincare purchasing were a consequence of colourism. S13 S14

How are family members protected online?

Only photographs approved for public exhibition should be published. Particular care is required where children appear. Public labels should describe the photograph and its occasion without publishing each person's assigned skin-shade roster. Consent should cover both the original and its transformed painting where possible; permissions should be revisited if a subject withdraws consent. Do not expose original EXIF location metadata, private full names, image training data, or individual face-colour assignments in public APIs.

How can viewers navigate this exhibition accessibly?

All artworks need meaningful alt text describing the grouping and the artistic intervention without assigning racial or complexion identities to pictured individuals. The legend must be provided as text; never rely on shade differences alone. Interactive features should work by keyboard, have captions/transcripts, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep the information readable without animation or hover.

Rights and historical material

Every original advertisement needs a source credit and verified reuse terms. Academic screenshots, advertisements still owned by brands, press photographs and television footage should remain external source links until permission or a carefully reviewed quotation exception is documented.
