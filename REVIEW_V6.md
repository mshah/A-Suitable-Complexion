# A Suitable Complexion — V6 wedding series / curatorial review

There are **17 candidates**: **5 working studies**, **12 artist-selected final images**. The earlier *Home / 06* beach family portrait has been **removed from the review gallery and asset folder**, as explicitly requested. The final publication edit is not decided. The image masters are preserved without editing or repainting.

## Central thesis

**The clothing celebrates an individual. The complexion chart converts them into a number.**

All the source photographs were taken at weddings. That connects the paintings directly to the matrimonial-biodata terminology behind the seven-category complexion scale. Wedding clothing represents extraordinary individuality, labour, culture, celebration, and personal presentation; the complexion system imposes a reductive standardized classification over the very same bodies. The wedding itself is both a site of family belonging and the cultural institution through which complexion preferences can be reproduced.

The original overarching question remains: **Why should someone have to purchase what should never have been conditional—dignity, possibility and belonging?**

## Curatorial proposal

**14 main-show candidates** and **3 reserve candidates**, all retained in this review.

Opening act: **I / The occasion** — ceremonial scenes, brides, grooms, witness relationships.
Middle act: **II / The company** — friends, peers, expressions, vivid personal dress.
Final act: **III / The inheritance** — intergenerational groups and the passing down of categories.

The strongest final show would alternate large and small groups within these acts. Reserve is a suggested presentation decision, not a revocation of the artist's final-image designation.

## Images to hold in reserve — not delete

- **Generations** (previous ASC-04): overlaps with stronger intergenerational scenes; some painted faces carry numbers, contrary to the rule reserving numbers for unpainted skin.
- **Garden Gathering** (new final): lovely setting and colourful clothing, but no one is visually unpainted/numbered; requires a stronger intervention to justify an additional group portrait.
- **Family Table Portrait** (previous ASC-08): attractive but repeated group arrangement and no visible unpainted complexion; less precise as an illustration of the system.

## Publish-readiness check, separate from artist's final-image selection

- **Wedding Couple** and **Three Women** are artist-selected final images but currently appear fully painted throughout, with no unpainted/numbered subject. Consider one unfilled figure if every canvas is intended to demonstrate both visual states. This does not make them unapproved artworks.
- **Seaside Ceremony**: check that the small numbers attach only to skin, not to clothing or headwear in the background.
- **Generations** (reserve): clear numbering appears on filled skin; merits regeneration if promoted into the main show.
- **Wedding Ritual**: some small background figures look numbered; check placements against the exact rules.
- For all works, verify **single flat palette shade per filled person's visible skin** and consistent exact seven-swatch key. Photographic shadows, contour shading, and multiple shades within a face contradict the intended treatment.
- Verify permission from individuals who will be publicly identifiable, especially any children. These selections remain for **private review**, not public release.
