# A Suitable Complexion — Website Editorial & Research Assets

**Version 1.0 · 26 October 2026 · Editorial draft, not a live website**

This package is a website-ready copy and editorial research foundation for the digital art exhibition *A Suitable Complexion*. It follows the established family-portrait concept document and brings in verified academic, archival, market and public-health evidence. It is **not** a cleared gallery of family photographs or a set of licensed corporate advertisements.

## Citation conventions

`[S13]` refers to `data/source_registry.csv`, for example. Website rendering can turn the IDs into source popovers or footnotes. Numbers intentionally distinguish measured/reported historical facts, industry estimates and corporate claims.

## Caution about quantities

- Global skin-lightening revenue (Grand View Research) and India total skincare revenue (Vyansa Intelligence) have different category definitions, geographies and methodologies. They must **not** be graphed as equivalent.
- The global 2026 and 2030 figures are projections, not audited sales.
- The claim that lower-income households sacrifice nutrition for fairness sachets is not supported by the sources in this pack.
- A universal light-skin hiring premium in India is not established; one relevant 2022 experiment reported no statistically significant advantage in its design.
