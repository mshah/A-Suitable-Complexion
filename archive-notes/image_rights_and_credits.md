# Historical Advertising — Image Rights and Publication Register

The archive URLs below lead to historically documented advertising images and research sources. A source link is not automatic permission to republish. Before launch, obtain the scan directly from the archive/copyright holder under the applicable licence, add a citation and image-specific alt text, and preserve the advertiser/date/provenance.

## Do not conflate date and place

A c.1909 British Hazeline leaflet is evidence of whitening claims in the early international market, **not** a verified Indian advertisement. A 1929 British Hazeline poster is a separate artifact from the 1930 *The Hindu* advertisement. Spell this out in captions.

## Consent and metadata rule

Private family photographs supplied for production are **not** bundled in this public editorial export. Their original and transformed versions require image-specific approval from the artist and appropriate subjects, especially for children. Archive IDs and non-identifying captions should be stored separately from private names, complexion assignments and unredacted source files.
